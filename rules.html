Rules:

Scoreboard Rules

  • The teams are ranked on the sum of points earned from solving challenges on the scoreboard.
  • In the event of a tie, the team that reaches the score first will be ranked higher than the others.

General Code of Conduct

  • Respect all teams, participants, admins, and the event environment.
  • Report any issues or rule violations to event admins promptly.
  • Maintain good sportsmanship. Any behavior deemed unsporting will lead to disqualification.

Do Not Discuss Solutions or Ask for Hints

  • Hints will not be provided. Do not spam admins for hints.
  • Do not discuss event challenge solutions or share flags with other teams. Flag sharing will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • Do not share challenge solutions before the event has ended.

Do Not Fuzz or Port Scan

  • The challenge descriptions will specify the host and port(s). Do not perform port scanning unless it is explicitly allowed in the challenge details.
  • If a target host is down or unavailable, please do adequate testing before reaching out. We will restore it as fast as possible, but it may cost you valuable participation time.
  • Do not overload systems with unnecessary requests. Stay focused on the challenge requirements.
  • Automating requests to this platform is strictly prohibited.

Not In Scope / Off Limits / Do Not Attack

  • This platform and its related infrastructure
  • Other participants' infrastructure
  • Any network or system other than what is specified in the challenge descriptions

Communication and Collaboration

  • Keep communication to your team members only. Collaboration with other teams is strictly prohibited unless explicitly allowed in the challenge description.

Right to Terminate

  • Event organizers reserve the right to terminate any participant's access to the event at any time for violating these rules or for any behavior that is deemed inappropriate or harmful to the event.
  • Termination decisions are final and not subject to appeal.

Failure to follow these rules may lead to immediate disqualification or removal from the event. Play fair and have fun!
